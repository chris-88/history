How to read this: Ancient and prehistoric entries often denote a culture or process, not a precisely dated occurrence. “c.” indicates an approximate date; dates and interpretations may be debated. There is no year zero in the historical BCE/CE calendar (the graphic uses an evenly spaced numeric scale for navigation). Modern place names are used as geographic labels for historical comparison, not as claims of uninterrupted national identity. Event descriptions are curated summaries; Wikipedia is provided as a jumping-off point for deeper reading, rather than as a substitute for primary research. This is a selective overview, not a claim that nothing else happened in blank years.
